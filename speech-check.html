Speech Check

Can the phone hear your Japanese?

Twenty short phrases. Say each one out loud and we will show you what the speech recogniser heard. About five minutes.

  • 1Find a quiet spot and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  • 2If you cannot read the Japanese, tap Listen first and hear the phrase. Use it whenever you need it — it never plays by itself.
  • 3Then tap Speak and say the phrase at your normal speed. Speak up and hold the phone near your mouth — a quiet voice is often not heard.
  • 4If nothing is heard, tap Speak and try once more, then move on.

Works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Samsung Internet. Firefox cannot do speech recognition. Your recording is never stored — only the text the recogniser produced.
